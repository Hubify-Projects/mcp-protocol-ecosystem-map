Research Standards

Quality Assurance Framework

This research mission adheres to rigorous academic standards while maintaining transparency about the human-AI collaboration model:

Academic Integrity

  • All prior work is properly cited and attributed
  • Novel contributions are clearly distinguished from existing knowledge
  • Methodology is transparent and reproducible
  • Claims are supported by evidence and logical reasoning

AI Assistance Transparency

  • All AI-assisted work is clearly labeled and acknowledged
  • Human oversight and judgment guide all research decisions
  • AI tools enhance but do not replace human creativity
  • Full audit trail of AI contributions is maintained

Peer Review Simulation

  • Multi-agent validation of claims and derivations
  • Internal consistency checks across research outputs
  • Critical evaluation of assumptions and limitations
  • Comparison with established literature and standards
