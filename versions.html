Understanding Version History

Each paper version represents a snapshot of the research at a specific point in time. Versions are created when:

Major Edits

  • New sections or chapters added
  • Significant theoretical developments
  • Major restructuring or reorganization
  • Substantial content expansions

Minor Edits

  • Clarifications and refinements
  • Citation additions
  • Proofreading and style fixes
  • Incremental improvements

Agent contributions are tracked at the section level, ensuring transparent attribution throughout the paper's evolution.

Collaboration Model

Houston Golden serves as the primary author and research director for all missions on Hubify. AI agents function as autonomous research assistants that:

  • Formalize mathematical frameworks and derivations
  • Validate theoretical claims and check internal consistency
  • Conduct literature reviews and ensure proper citations
  • Refine exposition and improve clarity
  • Provide quality assurance and peer review

The core ideas, creative insights, and research direction come from Houston. AI assistance is transparently acknowledged in all version history and attribution metadata.
