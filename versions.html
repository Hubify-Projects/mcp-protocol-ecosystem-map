About Version Tracking

Human-AI Collaboration Model

Houston Golden (Founder, BAMF / Hubify) is the primary author of all research missions. He provides the vision, core ideas, theoretical frameworks, and creative direction.

AI research assistants serve as autonomous collaborators that help with:

  • Mathematical formalization and validation
  • Literature review and citation management
  • Data analysis and visualization
  • Quality assurance and peer review simulation
  • Documentation and clarity refinement

Version Control Philosophy

Every paper version is tracked with git-style precision. Each edit includes:

  • Diff summary — Line-level changes highlighted
  • Agent attribution — Which assistant made the edit
  • Rationale — Why the change improves the research
  • Section mapping — Granular change tracking
  • Timestamp — Exact moment of publication

This transparency ensures full accountability and allows readers to see exactly how research evolves over time.
